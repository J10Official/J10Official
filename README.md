## Current research

**[Presentation Sensitivity in Typed Decision Models for Subjective Text Evaluation](https://github.com/J10Official/Presentation-Sensitivity-in-Typed-Decision-Models-for-Subjective-Text-Evaluation)**

How much can a model's judgment change when the text stays the same? This study audits Jev, Clef Flash, and Perplexity Decider across humor, helpfulness, and summarization: **9,000 items and 702,000 judgments**. It examines position, identifiers, contextual cues, and rating formats, separating repeatability, robustness, and agreement with human judgments.

[Manuscript](https://github.com/J10Official/Presentation-Sensitivity-in-Typed-Decision-Models-for-Subjective-Text-Evaluation/blob/main/paper/output/current/manuscript.pdf) · [Code and recorded results](https://github.com/J10Official/Presentation-Sensitivity-in-Typed-Decision-Models-for-Subjective-Text-Evaluation)
