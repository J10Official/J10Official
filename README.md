I’m an **AI researcher and engineer** working on language models, AI agents, and computational creativity. I study how models **generate, judge, and interact**, combining model training and adaptation with rigorous evaluation. I build across the stack: from preparing data and designing experiments to developing inference pipelines and interactive applications people can use.

At **IIIT Hyderabad’s Language Technologies Research Center (LTRC)**, I work with **Prof. Radhika Mamidi** on computational creativity and language model evaluation. My broader engineering work includes **Mixture-of-Experts architectures, multi-agent learning systems, offline RAG, and generative storytelling**. 

Previously, as **AI Research Lead at Telepathic Software**, I led evaluation and analysis for [**The Humor Tax**](https://laugh.so/research/humor-tax/), studying how post-training affects humor quality and diversity through human evaluation and calibrated model judging.

<h3 align="center">Presentation sensitivity in typed decision models</h3>

How much does a model’s judgment change when the same text is presented differently? An audit of **Jev, Clef Flash, and Perplexity Decider** across humor, helpfulness, and summarization: **9,000 items, six datasets, and 702,000 recorded judgments**. The release separates repeatability, presentation robustness, and agreement with people, and includes predictions and offline result reproduction.

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/J10Official/Presentation-Sensitivity-in-Typed-Decision-Models-for-Subjective-Text-Evaluation/blob/main/paper/output/current/manuscript.pdf">Manuscript</a> · <a href="https://github.com/J10Official/Presentation-Sensitivity-in-Typed-Decision-Models-for-Subjective-Text-Evaluation">Code & results</a></p>

<h3 align="center">Does Bigger Mean Funnier?</h3>

**Evaluating Humor Generation Across the Qwen3 Model Family.** A study of how model scale relates to humor quality across **five Qwen3 models, from 8B to 235B parameters**, using human and automated evaluation.

<p align="center"><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.chum-1.7/">Paper</a> · <a href="https://github.com/J10Official/Humor-Parameter-Ablation">Code</a></p>

<h3 align="center">Neurosymbolic humor generation</h3>

**GTVH-Guided LLM Decomposition · j10official at SemEval-2026 Task 1.** A five-stage **DSPy pipeline** that translates humor theory into a structured generation process. **Second place in both Chinese humor generation and GIF fill-in-the-blank.**

<p align="center"><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.semeval-1.379/">Paper</a> · <a href="https://github.com/J10Official/Semeval2026_Task1">Code</a></p>
